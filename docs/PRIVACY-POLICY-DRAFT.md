# TrackField privacy policy — draft, not for publication

This draft requires the operator to verify the actual deployment, identify the legal entity, document processors and backups, and implement and verify its retention and deletion procedures before it is published.

TrackField handles account information (such as name, email, role and bank association), authentication information, messages and attachments, ticket and workspace activity, and records relating to equipment and work. Where users provide location, it may be stored with attendance or work updates. Optional notifications use push subscription information. Cookies support sign-in, and browser storage saves some app preferences and location lookup results.

Location lookups use a configurable geocoding service; the code defaults to OpenStreetMap Nominatim. The operator must verify the production provider and accurately explain what information is sent to it. The operator must also identify hosting, notification and other service providers actually used, their roles and any relevant transfers.

Users can request account and associated data deletion by emailing sw-zihadul@networld-bd.com. The owner has specified 30 days for retention; the exact records, start point, exceptions and treatment of backups and shared records need confirmation. The existing application does not establish or enforce a 30-day deletion schedule. Do not claim it does until the process has been implemented and checked.

Before publication, add the legal operator's identity and contact, purposes and legal basis where applicable, access and sharing, retention periods and exceptions, user rights and how to exercise them, security practices, and policy update information. Verify every statement against the deployed service and applicable requirements.
